# Personal Data Request

You can ask Saver to review, update, export, or delete personal information associated with your account. Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to restrict or object to certain processing.

## Submit a request

Send your request to [support@saver-app.dev](mailto:support@saver-app.dev?subject=Personal%20data%20request). To help us locate your account, email us from the address associated with your Saver account whenever possible and tell us which action you want us to take.

Please do not include your password, payment details, or other sensitive information in your message.

We may need to verify your identity before completing the request. Information supplied for verification will only be used to confirm your identity or authority to make the request. We will consider and respond to requests in accordance with applicable data-protection laws.

You can also update available account information or request account deletion from Saver's account settings.

For more information, read our [Privacy Policy](/privacy).
