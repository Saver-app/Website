# Personal Data Request

Last updated August 7, 2026

You can ask Saver to review, update, export, or delete personal information associated with your account. Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to restrict or object to certain processing.

## Submit a request

Send your request to [support@saver-app.dev](mailto:support@saver-app.dev?subject=Personal%20data%20request). To help us locate your account, email us from the address associated with your Saver account whenever possible and tell us which action you want us to take.

Please do not include your password, payment details, or other sensitive information in your message.

We may need to verify your identity before completing the request. Information supplied for verification will only be used to confirm your identity or authority to make the request. We will consider and respond to requests in accordance with applicable data-protection laws.

You can also update available account information or request account deletion from Saver's account settings.

## Before deleting an account

- Account deletion does not cancel a subscription. Cancel it through the provider used for the purchase.
- Signing out or deleting an online account does not automatically erase offline spaces, app databases, browser-extension preferences, or provider caches on your devices. Delete local content and clear or uninstall the relevant client if you also want to remove those copies.
- Some nested cloud records may require separate server-side cleanup after an automated account or space deletion. Ask us to verify remote erasure if you want those related records inspected.
- Content contributed to a shared space owned by someone else may remain where needed to preserve that workspace, subject to applicable deletion and anonymisation rights.
- Apple, Google, Paddle, RevenueCat, Discord, and other providers may retain transaction, security, or platform records under their own legal obligations and policies.

If Saver's automated controls do not cover the data you want to access or remove, describe it in your request so we can assess it manually.

For more information, read our [Privacy Policy](/privacy).
