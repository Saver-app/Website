# Return & Refund Policy

Last updated August 7, 2026

## Statutory withdrawal for web purchases

For a Saver web purchase sold through Paddle, Paddle is the authorised reseller and seller for the transaction. Paddle's [Buyer Terms](https://www.paddle.com/legal/buyer-terms) and [Refund Policy](https://www.paddle.com/legal/refund-policy) explain applicable statutory withdrawal rights. Paddle currently directs buyers to exercise a withdrawal right through the **View receipt** or **Manage subscription** link in the transaction email, the support link in the receipt or billing page, or [Paddle Buyer Support](https://paddle.net/) using **Request refund**.

Submit the request before the applicable deadline and keep Paddle's electronic confirmation. The legal effect of beginning a digital service during the withdrawal period depends on the law that applies and the request, consent, and acknowledgement collected at checkout. Nothing in this policy treats ordinary use of Saver as a blanket waiver of a withdrawal right.

For an Apple App Store, Google Play, or Discord purchase, the receipt identifies the seller and the platform process through which a withdrawal or refund request must be made. See the channel links below.

## Voluntary 14-day refund

For purchases made from Paddle through Saver's web checkout, you may request a refund within 14 days of the original purchase date or, for a subscription, within 14 days of the most recent renewal date. Paddle acts as the authorised reseller for these transactions. This is a voluntary Saver refund policy in addition to any mandatory rights you have under applicable law.

To request a refund, email [support@saver-app.dev](mailto:support@saver-app.dev) from the address associated with your purchase and include enough information for us to identify the transaction, or submit the request through [Paddle Buyer Support](https://paddle.net/). A request made within the 14-day window will be approved unless the transaction has already been refunded or the request is fraudulent or abusive. Paddle processes the refund, and access associated with the refunded purchase may end.

## Apple App Store, Google Play, and Discord purchases

Apple, Google, and Discord provide the initial refund process for purchases they process or resell. Submit your request through the provider you used:

- [Request an Apple refund](https://support.apple.com/118223);
- [Request a Google Play refund](https://support.google.com/googleplay/workflow/9813244); or
- review [Discord's Refund Policy](https://support.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/360012668071-Refund-Policy) and contact Discord Billing.

The provider will determine eligibility under its policy and applicable law. If the provider directs you to Saver, or your issue is not covered by its policy, email [support@saver-app.dev](mailto:support@saver-app.dev).

## Other statutory rights

This policy does not limit any statutory withdrawal, refund, price-reduction, repair, replacement, or termination right. A statutory withdrawal right and our voluntary 14-day refund policy are separate. For digital content or services supplied during a withdrawal period, your withdrawal right expires or a proportional payment becomes due only if the legal requirements are met and any required express request, consent, and acknowledgement have been obtained.

This update does not reduce a voluntary refund right that already attached to a purchase under an earlier version of this policy.

Cancelling a subscription stops future renewals but does not automatically create a refund right. See section 9 of our [Terms of Service](/terms#returnno) for cancellation instructions.
