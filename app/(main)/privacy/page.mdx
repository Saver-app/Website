## 1. Scope and data sources

This policy applies when personal data is transmitted to Saver or a provider used to deliver the Services. Some Saver features can be used offline. Content that remains only on your device is not received by Saver until you choose to publish, sync, share, or otherwise transmit it.

We receive data:

- directly from you when you create an account, add content, buy a plan, connect an account, or contact us;
- from your device and browser when they communicate with the Services;
- from Google, Apple, Discord, RevenueCat, Paddle, and an app store when you use their account, integration, or purchase services;
- from collaborators, space owners, server administrators, or other people who add you to a space or use a Saver feature involving you; and
- from the browser extension or Discord bot when you deliberately invoke a save or integration feature.

We do not buy personal data from data brokers.

## 2. Data Saver processes

The data depends on the features you use.

### Account and authentication data

We may process your Firebase user ID, email address, display name, profile-photo URL, authentication provider, email-verification status, and account creation and last-sign-in times.

If you use email and password authentication, the credentials are submitted to Firebase Authentication. Saver does not store or have access to a readable copy of your password.

### Saver content and settings

Saver may process:

- space names and settings;
- task or todo text, status, hierarchy, and timestamps;
- bookmark and folder titles, full URLs, hierarchy, and timestamps;
- habit names, descriptions, schedules or requirements, streaks, completion dates, and counts;
- other free-form content supported by the feature and version you use;
- creator, editor, owner, and member identifiers;
- app preferences, active or default spaces and folders, feature settings, plan limits, and sync state; and
- local records and deletion or synchronisation metadata needed for offline operation.

Saver does not ask you to provide special-category data such as health, religious, political, or sexual information. Free-form tasks, habits, bookmarks, shared content, and Discord messages can nevertheless contain such information if a user chooses to enter or save it. Avoid adding sensitive personal data unless it is necessary and you are authorised to do so. Saver does not use the contents of your items to build advertising profiles.

### Collaboration and invitation data

For shared spaces, we may process member user IDs and display names, roles, permissions, owner status, joined and updated times, space identifiers, invitation tokens and related security values, expiry and use limits, and invitation-use records.

### Browser-extension and share data

When you open a signed-in Saver browser-extension popup, the extension may automatically save the active tab if a destination is configured. It processes the tab title and full URL, as well as the Saver account, space, folder, preference, and recent-bookmark data needed to perform the feature.

When you use an operating-system share sheet, Saver receives only the content you choose to send to it. Flutter-based clients can handle shared text, URLs, or a supported attachment reference and may save the result as a bookmark at your request. The current Swift share extension reads shared text, including a web URL represented as text, on the device and does not save it to Saver or transmit it to Saver's servers.

If you copy an invitation, Saver places the invitation link or token on the system clipboard. An app or person you choose through the operating system's share interface receives the selected invitation or content and applies its own privacy practices.

### Discord data

When you link Discord, install the bot, or invoke a bot feature, we may process Discord and Saver user IDs, username and global display name, guild and channel IDs, message and application IDs, permissions, commands, modal input, interaction tokens, and relevant timestamps.

For a message command, Discord provides the selected message. Saver may process its text, links, embeds, components, attachment names, URLs, and supported attachment content to carry out the command. If you customise a bot for a guild, we may also process the guild ID, updater's Discord ID, nickname, biography, avatar, banner, and update time.

### Purchase and subscription data

Saver may receive and store a provider customer or app-user ID, product and plan, entitlement status, transaction or event identifier, purchase and renewal status, environment, limits, currency or price information, and processing timestamps.

Saver does not receive your full payment-card number or card security code. Payment credentials are collected by the seller, app store, or payment provider.

### Website, device, and security data

When a device requests a website, API, authentication, database, or bot endpoint, Saver and its infrastructure providers may process the IP address, request date and time, requested URL, referrer where supplied, browser or app user agent, operating system or app version, language, response status, traffic-routing information, and security or error logs.

Firebase may process installation or app identifiers, authentication logs, and App Check tokens or attestation material. On the web app, reCAPTCHA v3 evaluates technical and interaction signals to help distinguish legitimate use from abuse. Saver does not use that assessment for advertising or to make decisions with legal or similarly significant effects.

If you install or update Saver for Android through Google Play, Google may process information about the app, your device, your Google account, the installation or update, and related activity under [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

Saver does not operate advertising, Firebase Analytics, Google Analytics, cross-app tracking, or a third-party crash-reporting service in the audited mobile apps and extensions. Provider SDKs may still process limited diagnostic, usage, and user or device identifier data for their own service operation and provider analytics, as described in their privacy manifests and notices.

### Support and transactional communications

If you contact us, we process your name, email address, topic, message, attachments or other information you provide, and the correspondence required to respond. Email delivery may involve your address, the message content, and an account-verification, password-reset, or other action link.

## 5. Google, Apple, and Discord account connections

Saver supports email and password, Google, and Apple sign-in. We do not offer Facebook or X sign-in and do not request a social-network friends list.

Google or Apple may provide a provider user identifier, name, email address, profile image or related authentication information shown in the authorisation flow. Apple may provide name and email only on the first authorisation. Authentication tokens are exchanged with Firebase to sign you in; Saver does not receive your Google or Apple password.

Optional Discord account linking in the main Saver apps requests Discord's basic `identify` scope. The connection can provide a Discord user ID, username, and global display name. Saver briefly uses the OAuth code and access token to complete the link, then stores the Saver-to-Discord account mapping and link timestamps. Disconnecting Discord removes that mapping from Saver's active account-link records; Discord may retain its own authorisation records.

## 6. Shared spaces and invitations

Content in a shared space is visible to authorised members and may be edited, assigned, moved, or deleted according to their permissions. Space owners and administrators can see membership data and manage roles and permissions. Depending on the client version, member records can expose full Saver user IDs to the space owner.

Anyone who has a valid invitation link may be able to retrieve limited invitation-preview information, such as the space name, before signing in. Link-preview systems may also request that information. Preview responses can be cached briefly. Do not publish an invitation link where unintended recipients can access it.

If you leave a space or delete your account, content you contributed to a space owned by someone else may remain so the shared workspace continues to function. The content may continue to contain your contributor identifier unless it is removed or anonymised. You can ask us to assess a deletion or anonymisation request through the methods in section 14.

Only invite people and add information about them when you have authority to do so. Other members are independent recipients of information you choose to share with them.

## 7. Browser extensions and share features

Saver offers or develops extensions for Chrome, Edge, Firefox, and Safari. The extension is designed to process a tab or link only when you activate a Saver feature. If you are signed in and have configured a destination, opening the popup can automatically save the current tab's title and full URL. If you choose **Save link as bookmark** from the browser's context menu, the extension processes the link URL and available link text, selected text, or referring-page URL to prepare a local bookmark draft. The extension does not continuously collect your general browsing history for advertising or profiling.

The current Firefox package declares a content-script match for all website URLs, so Firefox may display a broad website-access permission. The script's sign-in bridge accepts account messages only from `app.saver-app.com`; the broad match is not used to read page content or browsing history. This permission should be narrowed in a future extension build where technically possible.

The extension also reads the minimum Saver account, membership, space, folder, preference, and bookmark data needed to show destinations and recent saves. The Saver web app can pass a short-lived custom Firebase token to the installed extension through an origin-checked message so the extension can sign in.

Extension-local sign-in and preference data is stored as described in section 4. Removing the extension or clearing its data removes that local copy, but it does not delete bookmarks already synced to Saver.

The Safari extension's purchase interface can send your Saver user ID to RevenueCat and Apple to fetch offerings, purchase, restore, and associate entitlements. A temporary local handoff file used for this purpose is time-limited and deleted after consumption.

Native share features receive only content you deliberately select through the operating system. Third-party apps or websites you share from remain responsible for their own processing.

## 8. Discord bot and dashboard

The Saver bot responds to signed Discord interactions and does not continuously listen to every server conversation. Discord provides command input and, for a message context-menu command, the specific message you selected.

The separate dashboard sends the guild or channel destination and message content you enter to its API only when you submit the delivery request. Its Discord OAuth scopes and browser storage are described in sections 4 and 5.

Depending on the command, Saver may:

- store selected message text as a todo;
- scan selected text, embeds, attachments, or components for a link and store the resulting bookmark title and URL;
- copy selected message text, embeds, attachment content, source identifiers, and the saving user's identity to a chosen Discord direct message or channel; or
- read your permitted Saver spaces, folders, todos, bookmarks, habits, plan, and settings to show or update the requested result.

Most personal command responses are configured as ephemeral Discord responses. A message deliberately copied to a channel or direct message remains subject to that destination's audience and Discord's retention and deletion controls. A channel administrator or recipient may also copy it independently.

Guild appearance features may store a custom nickname, biography, avatar, banner, guild identifier, updater identifier, and update time in Firestore and send the customisation to Discord. Discord may record an audit-log reason containing the updater's Discord identity.

Selected messages and attachments can contain another person's personal or sensitive information. Use these commands only when you have authority to process and copy that content. Saver may log errors needed to diagnose a failed bot request; do not use the bot to transmit secrets.

## 9. Purchases and subscriptions

The provider shown at checkout processes the purchase:

- Paddle is the authorised reseller for supported Saver web purchases and collects the payment and contact information needed for checkout, tax, fraud prevention, receipts, cancellations, and refunds;
- Apple handles App Store and StoreKit payment credentials;
- Google handles Google Play payment credentials;
- Discord handles Discord Premium App payment credentials when that purchase channel is offered; and
- RevenueCat manages product offerings, receipts, subscription status, and entitlements across supported channels.

Saver sends RevenueCat a Saver or anonymous app-user ID and associates the Firebase user ID after sign-in. Saver receives enough subscription and event data to grant the correct plan and enforce its limits. This can link purchase history and entitlement status to your Saver account even if a provider's generic SDK disclosure describes the data as unlinked.

Deleting a Saver account does not automatically cancel a subscription or erase transaction records held by an independent seller, app store, or payment provider. Cancel through the purchase channel as described in the [Terms of Service](/terms#returnno).

## 12. Retention and deletion

We keep data only for as long as needed for the purpose described, taking account of account status, user controls, shared-content integrity, security, disputes, provider rules, backup cycles, and legal requirements.

- **Account and synced content:** generally kept while the account or content remains active, then deleted or de-identified after a valid deletion action or request, subject to the exceptions below and technical backup or cleanup cycles. Some nested cloud records can require separate server-side cleanup after an automated control removes the active parent account or space record. A verified erasure request asks us to inspect and complete that cleanup.
- **Shared content:** content contributed to another person's space may remain with the space after you leave or delete your account. We assess valid deletion and anonymisation requests against other members' rights and legal obligations.
- **Local and extension data:** offline spaces, app databases, Firestore caches, preferences, provider caches, extension preferences, and other local copies can remain after sign-out. Delete local spaces and items, use any available reset control, clear the app or extension data, or uninstall the relevant client. Signing out alone is not a device-data erasure request.
- **Authentication:** Firebase retains authentication records while the account exists. Firebase states that logged authentication IP addresses are kept for a few weeks and that other authentication data is removed from live and backup systems within up to 180 days after the customer initiates deletion.
- **Invitations and security records:** an invitation's expiry prevents further valid use, but invitation, abuse-prevention, event, and audit records may remain for security, quota enforcement, dispute handling, and legal compliance until no longer needed.
- **Discord:** an active account mapping and its bot preferences are removed when you disconnect Discord or complete account deletion. Saver items created from Discord remain until deleted like other Saver content. Copies sent back to Discord remain under Discord and channel controls. Guild appearance settings can remain until they are reset or no longer needed for the guild feature.
- **Purchases:** Saver and payment providers retain purchase, entitlement, refund, tax, fraud, and accounting records for their legitimate and statutory periods. Provider records are not erased merely because a Saver account is deleted.
- **Support:** we retain correspondence for as long as needed to answer the request, maintain an appropriate support history, resolve disputes, and comply with law. Web3Forms states that form submissions may be retained for up to three years unless deleted sooner or a shorter plan period applies.
- **Technical logs:** hosting, authentication, function, bot, CDN, and security providers keep logs for their documented operational or security periods; records connected to an incident or claim may be retained longer where necessary.

Account deletion is separate from subscription cancellation. Before deleting an account or local app data, keep your own copy of content you need. Saver does not currently provide a complete automated export in every client, but you may request a copy through section 14.
